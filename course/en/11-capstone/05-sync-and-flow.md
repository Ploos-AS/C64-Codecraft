# Music sync and flow
Turn cues into production structure.

Use the player's documented state/cue interface or your explicit integration counter. Drive transitions, palette changes, effect phases or part changes from intentional events.

Do not scrape undocumented player internals and call the result an interface.

Watch the whole production repeatedly. Technical correctness is necessary, but pacing is experienced over time.

## Gate
Important transitions are deterministic and their cue source is documented. The production reaches the same intended structure on repeated runs.

Measure the real build and optimize only what limits it.
