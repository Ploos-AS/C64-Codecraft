# Concept and scope
Start with a production idea, not a technology checklist.

Write a one-page brief covering:
- title/working title;
- visual and musical direction;
- intended duration;
- target machine/video standard;
- one-file or multipart structure;
- major parts/transitions;
- effects that serve the concept;
- asset authorship/provenance plan;
- technical risks;
- a deliberately small minimum shippable version.

## Scene connection
**Why does a demo coder care about this?**
The best technical trick cannot rescue a production whose scope never converges.

## Gate
Do not begin the final implementation until the minimum release can be described clearly.
