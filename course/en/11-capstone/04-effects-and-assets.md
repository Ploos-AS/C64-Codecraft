# Effects and assets
Implement the production's chosen effects because they support the concept, not because the course mentioned them.

At least one substantial element should require genuine hardware reasoning: raster timing, sprite scheduling, scrolling/DYCP, bitmap/high-colour work or another documented VIC-II/6510 technique.

Integrate graphics and music through reproducible pipelines. Record provenance immediately.

## Originality
Original does not mean inventing a never-before-seen VIC-II trick. It means making your own production decisions, code integration, presentation and combination rather than submitting the course examples unchanged.

## Gate
Every visible/audible asset has known provenance and every major effect can be explained from hardware behaviour.
