# $D011 and $D012 — the raster counter
$D012 carries the low eight raster-line bits; the raster high bit is associated with bit 7 of $D011. $D011 also contains display-control bits, so do not overwrite its whole byte casually.

```asm
wait:
    lda $d012
    cmp #target_line
    bne wait
    lda #$06
    sta $d020
```

Polling teaches synchronization, but branch timing, interrupts and VIC-II bus activity mean this is not yet stable raster code.

## Scene connection
**Why does a demo coder care about this?**
$D011/$D012 are the doorway from ordinary code into display synchronization.

## Lab
Poll a line within the low-byte range and change the border. Observe how other activity affects the transition.
