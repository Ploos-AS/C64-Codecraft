# Appendix E — Graphics and music tools

Scene production commonly mixes assembler work with specialized asset tools.

## Graphics
Useful categories include sprite editors, charset editors, bitmap/high-colour editors and converters. The exact application is replaceable; the exported C64 format and reproducible conversion contract matter more than the GUI.

## Music
GoatTracker-style SID workflows are useful for learning how authored music becomes player/data consumed by assembly code. Player interfaces remain player-specific: document init/play/cues, memory use, clobbers and playback cadence.

## Asset rule
Preserve editable legal source assets where licensing permits. Record tool/version/export settings and generated format. Never make an opaque binary export the only explanation of how an asset reaches the C64.

## Provenance
Record author/handle, source, license/permission, modifications and required credit for third-party material.
