# Lab 04.02 — First SID voice

## Goal
Configure one SID voice explicitly: frequency, envelope and waveform/gate.

The constants are teaching values, not a claim about concert pitch across every SID/video-clock combination. Change one field at a time and listen.

## Observe
Relate $D400/$D401, $D405/$D406 and $D404 to frequency, ADSR and control.

**Why does a demo coder care?** Even when using existing music, knowing what a player changes helps with debugging, sync and resource conflicts.
