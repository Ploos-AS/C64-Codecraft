# Lab 11.09 — Capstone postmortem

## Goal
Turn one finished demo into reusable engineering knowledge.

After release, write a short postmortem covering:
- what the original concept became,
- strongest technical decision,
- largest integration failure,
- timing/memory assumptions that survived or changed,
- toolchain friction,
- optimization that mattered,
- optimization that did not,
- emulator versus hardware findings,
- what you would design differently next time,
- one technique to explore in the next production.

Keep concrete measurements, debugger findings and qualification notes where possible.

The starter writes a final marker to RAM; the real deliverable is the evidence-backed review.

**Why does a demo coder care?** Scene skill compounds when finished productions become data for the next one instead of disappearing into folklore.
